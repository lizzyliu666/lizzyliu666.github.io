Human-Centered AI

Emotion AIEthical AIResponsible AIHuman-AI Interaction

I reimagine AI that keeps people — their identities, emotions, and relationships — at the center. My work aim to design care-oriented and emotion-aware AI that support emotional expression. I investigate AI systems that align with care ethics and articulates ethical design guidelines so that AI strengthens rather than replaces human connection.

Multimodal Communication

Video Social MediaVisual CultureVision-Language ModelsComputational Methods

I study how multimodal media reshape the way people seek and share health information in everyday life. Using large vision–language models, I develop workflows to analyze video-based social media at scale, surfacing the disparities and risks that emerge in the visual-mediated communication. I aim to reveal the dynamics among visual norms, social media, and GenAI images at scale.

Health Behavioral Change & Well-Being

Behavioral ChangeMental HealthHelp-SeekingHealth Equity

I examine how people — especially marginalized communities — change health behaviors and navigate mental health care across digital and everyday settings. This line of work informs responsible technologies that support meaningful behavioral change for well-being.
