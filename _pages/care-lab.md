### Computing & AI for Responsible Engagement (CARE Lab)

Some research directions and example topics:

1. **Responsible AI for Wellbeing** — social robots for dementia care, embodied AI for relational emotion support, and related topics.
2. **Social Computing & Multimodal Platforms** — computational TikTok/video analysis, algorithmic visibility, and multimodal social media.
3. **Generative AI Auditing & Representation** — bias in generative AI images, representational harms, and related auditing questions.
4. **Other directions** that align with our shared interests.
   

I am looking for dedicated students with strong research interests and a genuine commitment to research to join the CARE Lab!

If you are interested, please fill out this form: <a href="https://tally.so/r/RGpPRl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://tally.so/r/RGpPRl</a>. For questions, feel free to contact me at jiayingliu@utexas.edu.
