# Computing & AI for Responsible Engagement (CARE Lab)

CARE Lab examines how AI and digital platforms shape wellbeing, social interaction, and public life, with a focus on designing technologies that support human flourishing rather than harm. Our work sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction, social computing, media studies, and responsible AI, and we study how emerging systems affect identity, care, emotion, visibility, and decision-making in everyday life.

We are especially interested in the design and evaluation of AI systems that are not only technically capable but also socially accountable, culturally sensitive, and supportive of people’s needs across health, care, and digital communities.

## Research directions

### 1. Responsible AI for Wellbeing

We study how AI can support human welfare in socially meaningful settings, including care contexts and relationship-centered interventions. Example topics include social robots for dementia care, embodied AI for relational emotion support, and AI systems that help people manage stress, loneliness, or seeking help in timely and supportive ways.

### 2. Social Computing & Multimodal Platforms

We investigate how people produce, consume, and are shaped by multimodal and platform-mediated communication. This includes computational analysis of TikTok and short-form video, algorithmic visibility and recommendation systems, and the social, emotional, and health implications of platform design and content circulation.

### 3. Generative AI Auditing & Representation

We examine the societal and representational risks of generative AI, including bias in generated images, stereotyped portrayals, and harms that arise from misrepresentation or erasure. We are interested in how generative systems reproduce social norms and whose voices, identities, and experiences are foregrounded or marginalized.

### 4. Other directions of shared interest

We are open to other research directions that connect AI, communication, and human well-being, especially where there is a clear problem of social meaning, ethics, or public impact. We welcome ideas that build on the themes above or explore new questions at the intersection of technology and human flourishing.

## Open positions

I am looking for dedicated students with strong research interests and a genuine commitment to research to join the CARE Lab. If you are interested, please submit your application through this form: <a href="https://tally.so/r/RGpPRl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://tally.so/r/RGpPRl</a>.

Please prepare the following in your submission:

- Paragraph 1 — Research fit: Briefly introduce your research interests and background, and explain how they overlap with my research.
- Paragraph 2 — Research ideas: Describe 1–3 research ideas you would be interested in exploring. These can build on the projects listed on this website or other phenomena/topics that genuinely interest you. Please keep each idea to no more than two sentences.
- Paragraph 3 — Preparation: Describe the skills, methods, or prior experiences that would help you carry out these ideas.
- Your CV attached.

One important thing I learned is the merit of clear and efficient communication in research collaboration, and part of what I want to pass to my students. As a first interaction, these tips can also be adapted for program applications or cover letters.

If you have questions after submitting the form, please feel free to reach out by email.
